




# Scenario A - The hospital

## A regional hospital is hit by ransomware. Patient records are encrypted on file servers, scheduling systems are offline, and the hospital diverts emergency arrivals to other facilities for 36 hours. The attackers also exfiltrated a sample of patient files and demand payment to prevent publication.
The main CIA triad principle affected in this scenario is availability. The ransomware encrypted patient records on the file servers and took the scheduling systems offline so the doctors could not access the data and systems they needed. The hospital even had to divert emergency arrivals to other facilities for 36 hours. In a hospital, losing access to records and systems directly endangers patient safety, which makes availability the most severe impact. Confidentiality is also affected. The attackers exfiltrated a sample of patient files and threaten to publish them unless the hospital pays. This means sensitive health data was accessed by unauthorized people, because the scenario does not say that any records were altered it isnt about integrity.
